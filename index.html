2026-07-03 · U.S. Supreme Court

SCOTUS rules 6-3 that for-cause removal protections for SEC and CFTC commissioners are unconstitutional (Trump v. Slaughter, No. 25-332)

On June 29, 2026, the Supreme Court of the United States issued its 6-3 opinion in Trump v. Slaughter (No. 25-332), authored by Chief Justice Roberts, overturning Humphrey's Executor v. United States (1935). The Court held that the for-cause removal protection in the FTC's enabling statute — and by structural extension the SEC and CFTC, which operate under identical removal provisions — is contrary to the separation of powers under the Constitution. Effective immediately, the President may remove SEC and CFTC commissioners without cause. A companion order in Trump v. Cook (No. 25A312, June 29, 2026) denied a government application to stay a preliminary injunction protecting Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook; the Federal Reserve's statutory structure was not directly addressed by the majority opinion. The opinion is published on the Court's official slip opinion list for October Term 2025.
