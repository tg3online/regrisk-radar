2026-06-26 · U.S. Senate (17 Democrats)

Senate Democrats ask Appropriations Committee to bar CFTC from funding state prediction market preemption suits

On June 26, 2026, seventeen Democratic senators — led by Senators Blumenthal (D-CT), Merkley (D-OR), Schiff (D-CA), and Padilla (D-CA), joined by Senators Cantwell, Cortez Masto, Durbin, Heinrich, Hickenlooper, Hirono, Luján, Murphy, Rosen, Schatz, Smith, Warren, and Whitehouse — sent a letter to the Senate Appropriations Subcommittee on Financial Services and General Government requesting that the Fiscal Year 2027 appropriations bill prohibit the Commodity Futures Trading Commission from using federal funds to prevent states and Tribes from enforcing their gambling laws and gaming compacts against online prediction markets. The letter identifies eight states already sued by the CFTC (Connecticut, Illinois, Arizona, Wisconsin, New York, Minnesota, Rhode Island, and New Mexico) and states: 'Through engaging in this campaign of litigation and intimidation, the CFTC risks becoming an instrument and enabler of online prediction markets' efforts to bypass states' consumer protections and oversight, creating a race-to-the-bottom in gambling.'
