2026-06-25 · CFTC

CFTC proposes formal data-reporting rules for event contracts, ending eight-plus years of no-action relief (NPRM 9261-26)

On June 25, 2026, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission published a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (Press Release 9261-26) proposing amendments to Parts 15, 16, and 17 of CFTC regulations to establish an alternate data-reporting framework for fully collateralized event contracts. The proposal would require reporting markets, futures commission merchants (FCMs), clearing members, and foreign brokers to report event contracts under Parts 15-18 of the CFTC's regulations rather than under Parts 38, 39, 43, and 45, replacing an informal reporting regime that has operated under staff no-action letters since 2017. A public comment period is open; the comment deadline will be set in the Federal Register notice.

Kalshi files federal lawsuit against Illinois over SB3019's 15% sports prediction market gross receipts tax and state licensing requirement for DCMs; TRO and PI sought ahead of July 1 effective date

Kalshi filed a 31-page federal complaint in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois on June 23, 2026, naming Governor JB Pritzker, Attorney General Kwame Raoul, and five Illinois Gaming Board officials as defendants. The complaint seeks a temporary restraining order, preliminary injunction, and permanent injunction.
